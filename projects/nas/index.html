For my 2026 Spring Semester EEP, I decided to begin running my own cloud server from home. Among other things, I intend to establish the data transfer speeds of older hardware.

I'm using a Lenovo Thinkcenter M710q running Debian 13 "Trixie". On that server, I'm using ZFS for the single storage drive that I have, NFS for transferring files over the network, and Tailscale for SSH access when I am away from home.
